A custom patio should feel like a natural extension of your home. At Lake County Outdoors, we design and build custom patios for homeowners who want more than a basic slab. We create outdoor living spaces that look sharp, last through Minnesota weather, and fit the way you actually use your yard. Whether you want a quiet spot for morning coffee, a clean space for grilling, or a full dream backyard for hosting, we build patios that match your goals.

Our custom patio service starts with planning. We look at your yard layout, grading, drainage, access, sun exposure, and how the patio will connect to your home and landscape. From there, we help shape the design, choose the right size, and recommend materials that fit both your style and budget. We regularly build patios with pavers, concrete, and integrated hardscape features that bring the whole space together.

Because we are a full-service landscaping and hardscaping team, we can do much more than just install the patio surface. We can include boulder walls, transitions to walkways, edging, and site grading to create a polished finished result. For homeowners planning a higher-end backyard transformation, we can help make sure the patio works as part of the bigger picture instead of feeling like a stand-alone add-on.

Installation matters just as much as design. A patio that is not built on a solid base can shift, settle, or hold water over time. Our crew focuses on proper site prep, strong base work, clean layout lines, and reliable installation methods so your patio is built for long-term performance. With 10+ years of combined experience, we understand how to build hardscapes that stand up to freeze-thaw cycles and heavy seasonal use in the Minneapolis metro area.

We are known for taking on large, high-end outdoor projects, but we also keep communication simple and direct. We walk you through the scope, explain the process, and work to deliver a finished patio that adds beauty, function, and value to your property. If you are ready to invest in a custom patio that upgrades your backyard and gives you a space you will use for years, contact Lake County Outdoors to start planning your project.
