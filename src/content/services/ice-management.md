Ice buildup can turn a parking lot, sidewalk, entryway, or loading area into a serious safety risk fast. At Lake County Outdoors, we provide commercial ice management services for properties across the Minneapolis metro to help reduce slip hazards, protect visitors and staff, and keep your site open and usable during winter weather.

We focus on proactive service, not just reacting after conditions get bad. Our ice management plans are built around site conditions, foot traffic, vehicle traffic, drainage patterns, shaded areas, and known trouble spots. That means we target the places where refreeze and black ice are most likely to create problems.

Our service can include pretreatment before a storm, deicing applications during active winter weather, and follow-up treatments after snow removal when surfaces start to refreeze. We manage ice on parking lots, drive lanes, sidewalks, walkways, entrances, ramps, and other high-risk access points. For commercial properties, this helps maintain safer access for employees, customers, tenants, and delivery drivers.

We typically provide this service through seasonal winter maintenance agreements, which allows for more consistent coverage throughout the season. Terms vary by property and site needs, but our goal is always the same: reliable service that supports safety, site function, and peace of mind. We especially welcome commercial accounts that need a dependable partner for ongoing winter property care.

Because snow and ice conditions can change quickly in Minnesota, experience matters. Our team brings 10+ years of combined hands-on experience in outdoor property services, and we understand how local winter conditions affect pavement, concrete, and pedestrian areas. We take a practical approach, communicate clearly, and work to keep your property in better shape through every storm cycle.

If you manage a commercial property in the Twin Cities and need dependable ice hazard management this winter, we are actively servicing commercial sites now. Contact Lake County Outdoors to discuss a seasonal ice management plan for your property.
