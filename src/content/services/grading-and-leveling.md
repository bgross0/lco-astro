Good grading is what makes the rest of your project work. At Lake County Outdoors, we handle grading and leveling to create a solid base for patios, sod, planting beds, drainage fixes, and full landscape installs. If the ground is not shaped the right way, you can end up with standing water, soil erosion, uneven surfaces, and hardscape failure down the road. We make sure that does not happen.

Our grading and leveling service starts with a close look at your property. We check slope, low spots, drainage patterns, soil conditions, and how the finished area needs to function. Then we build a plan to move, add, or reshape material so the site is smooth, stable, and ready for the next phase. This can include rough grading, finish grading, backfilling, soil distribution, base prep, and correcting problem areas around foundations, yards, and future hardscape features.

This service is often the first step in larger outdoor projects. We regularly complete grading before installing custom patios, boulder walls, concrete features, lawn areas, and other high-end landscaping elements. With 10+ years of combined experience, our team understands how important the base layer is to the long-term performance of your investment. A properly graded site helps improve drainage, supports healthy turf growth, and gives your outdoor space a cleaner, more polished look.

Homeowners across the Minneapolis metro call us when they want to fix a bumpy yard, prepare for a new build-out, address water runoff, or set the stage for a dream backyard project. We also help reshape spaces after tree removal or major site changes. Because we are a full-service outdoor company, we can look at the bigger picture and make sure the grading supports the final design, not just the current problem.

While grading and leveling is a warm-weather service, site conditions matter year-round in Minnesota. Water that is not directed properly can freeze, expand, and create bigger issues later. That is one reason we also stay focused on commercial snow removal and ice management during the winter season. We are actively servicing commercial properties this winter and helping clients keep sites safer, more accessible, and easier to manage in harsh conditions.

If you are planning a landscape or hardscape project and want it done right from the ground up, we are ready to help.
