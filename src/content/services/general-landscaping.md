At Lake County Outdoors, we use general landscaping to turn ordinary yards into clean, usable, great-looking outdoor spaces. Whether you want a simple refresh or the first step toward a full dream backyard, we design and build landscapes that fit your home, your routine, and the way you want to use your property.

Our general landscaping service can include site cleanups, grading, plant bed shaping, mulch and rock installation, edging, sod or seeding prep, and overall yard improvement work that helps your property look finished and well cared for. We focus on both appearance and function. That means better drainage, cleaner lines, easier upkeep, and outdoor areas that make more sense for daily living.

We are a full-service landscaping company serving the Minneapolis metro and the wider Twin Cities area. Our team brings 10+ years of combined hands-on experience, and we can take projects far beyond basic landscaping when needed. If your yard calls for bigger improvements, we also handle boulder walls, custom patios, concrete work, tree removal and trimming, and other hardscape features that help tie the whole property together.

For homeowners, general landscaping is often the foundation of a larger outdoor plan. We can help shape front yards for stronger curb appeal, improve backyard layouts for entertaining, and create practical spaces around patios, walkways, and planting areas. Every property is different, so we look at grading, traffic flow, sun exposure, drainage, and long-term maintenance before recommending the right approach.

While landscaping is a core part of what we do in the warmer months, we also want property owners to know that we are actively servicing commercial properties this winter with commercial snow removal and ice management. Many clients value working with one dependable outdoor contractor who understands their site year-round. Our winter services are geared toward commercial accounts and seasonal maintenance agreements, with a strong focus on reliable snow response and safer access during Minnesota weather.

If you are planning landscape improvements and want a team that can handle both outdoor upgrades and winter property support, we are ready to help. Contact Lake County Outdoors to talk through your project goals and get your property on the schedule.
