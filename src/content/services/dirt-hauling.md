When a project leaves behind extra soil, fill, clay, or debris, we make cleanup simple with fast, reliable dirt hauling. At Lake County Outdoors, we handle dirt removal for landscape projects, grading work, excavation cleanup, backyard transformations, and new outdoor construction. Whether you are clearing space for a patio, fixing drainage issues, removing spoils from trenching, or cleaning up after tree work, we help keep your site safe, clean, and ready for the next step.

Our dirt hauling service is built around efficiency. We load and remove unwanted dirt from residential and select commercial properties across the Minneapolis metro and Twin Cities area. We understand that excess soil can slow down a project, create access problems, and leave a property looking unfinished. Our goal is to get it out quickly so your install, restoration, or site prep can stay on schedule.

We often provide dirt hauling as part of larger landscaping and hardscaping work, including custom patios, boulder walls, concrete installations, grading, and yard reshaping. Because we are a full-service outdoor company with 10+ years of combined experience, we know how dirt removal fits into the bigger picture. We plan for access, protect surrounding areas as much as possible, and work carefully to reduce disruption to your lawn, driveway, and finished surfaces.

Customers call us for many types of hauling needs, including clean fill removal, heavy soil piles, excavation spoils, sod and dirt cleanup, and post-construction landscape debris mixed with soil. If your site has limited access or a tight work area, we can review the layout and recommend the best approach for safe and efficient removal.

We are known for large outdoor transformation projects, and that same practical jobsite experience helps us deliver dependable hauling support. If you need a team that understands site flow, timing, and cleanup—not just loading and leaving—we are ready to help.
