Winter weather can shut down a property fast. At Lake County Outdoors, we help commercial properties stay open, safer, and easier to access with dependable commercial snow removal and ice management across the Twin Cities metro. We are actively servicing commercial sites this winter and focus on seasonal snow agreements built around each property’s layout, traffic flow, and risk areas.

Our commercial snow service is designed for businesses, office buildings, retail centers, industrial properties, HOAs, and other managed sites that need reliable snow response. We handle snow plowing, snow pushing, clearing drive lanes, opening parking lots, cleaning access points, and managing slippery conditions with ice control. The goal is simple: keep your property usable for tenants, employees, customers, vendors, and emergency access.

Every site has different needs, so we build service plans around the details that matter. That can include lot size, entry and exit patterns, loading areas, sidewalks, high-traffic zones, stacking locations, and problem spots where drifting or refreezing often happens. We typically work under seasonal maintenance agreements for winter service, which gives our commercial clients a more consistent plan for ongoing snow and ice events throughout the season.

We bring 10+ years of combined experience in outdoor property work and understand how important timing, communication, and follow-through are during Minnesota winters. Our team is built to support commercial properties that need dependable service, and we also have CDL capability on staff to support winter operations. We prefer commercial accounts because they allow us to create a clear service strategy and deliver the level of consistency property managers expect.

Good snow management is about more than moving snow. It helps reduce slip hazards, supports smoother business operations, improves access, and protects your professional image during storms. A well-maintained property tells tenants and visitors that safety and upkeep are taken seriously.

If you need commercial snow removal in Minneapolis or the surrounding metro, now is the time to secure winter service. Contact Lake County Outdoors to discuss a seasonal snow and ice management plan for your property.

## Ice Management

Ice is the most dangerous — and most preventable — winter hazard on any property. Lake County Outdoors takes a proactive approach to ice control, treating surfaces before freezing rain, black ice, and overnight refreeze have a chance to create liability exposure. We don't wait for a call after someone slips; we're already out ahead of the forecast.

Our crews track pavement and surface temperatures alongside weather data to time liquid brine applications before a storm bonds ice to pavement, and follow up with granular de-icing material on walkways, entries, stairs, and high-traffic zones. Every application is logged — material type, quantity, time, and location — so you have a documented record for every property, every event. And because it's our own crews and our own equipment on your property every time, not a rotating cast of subcontractors, the same team that knows your site's problem areas is the one showing up at 4am to treat them.

### Highlights:

- Pre-storm liquid brine application to prevent ice bonding
- Granular ice melt for walkways, entries, stairs, and high-traffic areas
- 24/7 monitoring for refreeze conditions, especially overnight and early morning
- Time-stamped application logs for every site, every visit
- Environmentally conscious material selection near landscaping, storm drains, and water features
- Performed exclusively by LCO crews — never subcontracted

## Snow Hauling

When snow has nowhere left to go, it becomes a liability. Piled snowbanks block sightlines, eat up parking, bury fire lanes, and turn into slow-motion floods and refreeze hazards as they melt. Lake County Outdoors provides full snow hauling and off-site relocation to keep your property functional and compliant all winter long — not just plowed, but actually clear.

We monitor accumulation at every site and schedule hauling once snowbanks cross a defined height or volume threshold, with priority given to fire lanes, ADA-designated stalls, loading docks, and dumpster access to keep you code-compliant and operational. All snow is removed to approved off-site disposal locations. Because it's handled by the same LCO crews managing your plowing and ice control — not a separate hauling subcontractor — there's no coordination gap and no finger-pointing between vendors.

### Highlights:

- Scheduled hauling once snowbanks reach a defined threshold
- Priority clearing of fire lanes, ADA spaces, loading docks, and dumpster access
- Off-site disposal at approved snow storage locations
- Available on-demand or built into your seasonal contract
- Reduces liability from blocked sightlines, runoff, and refreeze in melt zones
- One crew, start to finish — no subcontracted hauling vendors
