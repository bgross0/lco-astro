At Lake County Outdoors, we build concrete features that make outdoor spaces more useful, durable, and clean-looking. Our concrete work is designed for homeowners who want a backyard that feels finished and lasts through Minnesota weather. Whether you are adding a new patio, replacing an old slab, or tying concrete into a larger hardscape design, we focus on solid prep, precise grading, and a clean final result.

We install concrete for patios, walkways, pads, outdoor living areas, and other custom hardscape surfaces. For many projects, concrete is the best choice when you want strength, simple maintenance, and a crisp, modern look. It also works well with other landscape features like retaining walls, boulder walls, fencing, planting beds, and drainage improvements. If you are planning a high-end dream backyard, concrete can serve as the foundation that brings the whole design together.

Our process starts with understanding how you want to use the space. We look at layout, traffic flow, grading, drainage, and how the new surface will connect to the rest of the property. Proper base preparation matters just as much as the finish you see on top. We take time to create a stable foundation, set forms carefully, and make sure the concrete is placed with attention to slope and long-term performance. That helps reduce common problems like standing water, uneven settling, and premature cracking.

Because we are a full-service landscaping and hardscaping company, we can handle more than just the slab itself. We can combine concrete work with custom patios, large hardscape builds, tree work, and full outdoor upgrades to create a more complete result. Our team brings 10+ years of combined hands-on experience, and we are known in the Minneapolis and Twin Cities metro for taking on substantial outdoor projects with a practical, detail-focused approach.

Even though concrete is part of our warm-weather service lineup, many customers first get to know us through our commercial snow removal and ice management work in winter. We actively service commercial properties across the Minneapolis metro this winter, and that same reliability, site planning mindset, and commitment to safety carry into every landscape and hardscape project we take on.

If you are ready to add a durable concrete patio, walkway, or outdoor surface to your property, we are ready to help you plan it and build it the right way.
