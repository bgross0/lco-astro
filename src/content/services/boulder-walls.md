A well-built boulder wall does more than hold back soil. It adds shape, texture, and lasting strength to your landscape. At Lake County Outdoors, we design and install boulder walls that look natural, solve grading problems, and help turn ordinary yards into standout outdoor spaces.

Boulder walls are a great fit for properties with slopes, erosion issues, uneven ground, or areas that need stronger support than smaller retaining materials can provide. We often use them to create level lawn space, frame patios, define planting beds, improve drainage flow, and build structure into a backyard design. They also pair well with other hardscape features like custom patios, concrete work, and full landscape installations.

Our process starts with a site review and a clear plan for the layout, height, placement, and purpose of the wall. We look at grading, soil conditions, access, water movement, and how the wall should fit the overall look of the property. From there, we select and place each boulder with care to create a wall that is both stable and visually balanced. Proper base prep, thoughtful stacking, and drainage planning all matter if you want a wall that performs well through Minnesota freeze-thaw cycles.

We build boulder walls for both function and appearance. Some projects need a heavy-duty retaining wall to manage earth pressure and protect usable space. Others are more decorative and are used to add natural stone character to a front yard or dream backyard. In many cases, the best result is a mix of both. We can build straight runs, curved walls, stepped areas, and integrated transitions that work with the shape of the land.

Our team brings 10+ years of combined experience in landscaping and hardscaping installations across the Minneapolis metro and Twin Cities area. We focus on larger, high-end outdoor projects, and we know how to coordinate boulder walls as part of a full landscape plan. If you want a strong, natural-looking solution that adds value and long-term stability to your property, we are ready to help. Contact Lake County Outdoors to talk through your project and schedule a consultation.
