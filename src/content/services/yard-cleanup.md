At Lake County Outdoors, our yard cleanup service is built to make your property look neat, healthy, and ready for the next season. Whether your yard is covered in fallen leaves, storm debris, overgrown edges, or leftover brush, we handle the hard work and clean it up the right way. A thorough cleanup does more than improve curb appeal. It helps protect your lawn, supports healthy plant growth, and gives you a cleaner, safer outdoor space to enjoy.

Our yard cleanup service can include leaf removal, stick and branch pickup, brush clearing, garden bed cleanup, weed removal, edging, and hauling away organic debris. We also clean up around fences, patios, walkways, and hardscape features so the whole property looks finished. If your yard has been neglected for a while, we can create a custom plan to get it back under control without cutting corners.

We bring 10+ years of combined hands-on experience to outdoor property care across the Minneapolis and Twin Cities metro area. Because we also handle landscaping, hardscaping installations, tree trimming, and tree removal, we know how to spot problem areas that can affect the overall health and appearance of your yard. That means we do more than basic cleanup. We pay attention to drainage concerns, damaged limbs, buildup around landscaping features, and other issues that can lead to bigger maintenance problems later.

Seasonal yard cleanup is especially helpful in spring and fall. In spring, we remove winter debris and prepare your lawn and landscape beds for healthy growth. In fall, we clear leaves and organic buildup that can smother grass and create a messy, slippery surface. For commercial properties, we also understand how outdoor maintenance connects with year-round site care. While yard cleanup helps keep grounds presentable in warmer months, we are also actively servicing commercial properties this winter with snow removal and ice management through seasonal agreements.

If you want a cleaner yard without spending your weekend doing the heavy lifting, we are ready to help. We show up prepared, work efficiently, and leave your property looking refreshed. Contact Lake County Outdoors to schedule your yard cleanup and get your outdoor space back in shape.
