Water problems in your yard do not fix themselves. They usually get worse over time and can lead to soggy lawns, standing water, soil erosion, mulch washout, plant damage, and even water getting too close to your foundation. At Lake County Outdoors, we provide drainage solutions that are built to move water away from the areas where it causes damage and toward a safe outlet.

We start by looking at how water moves across your property. That includes grading issues, low spots, gutter discharge, downspout runoff, hardscape runoff, and areas where water collects after rain or snowmelt. In the Minneapolis metro, freeze-thaw cycles and heavy seasonal runoff can create major drainage problems fast, so a proper plan matters.

Our drainage services may include yard regrading, swales, drain tile, French drains, catch basins, channel drains, downspout extensions, drainage corrections around patios and walkways, and erosion control solutions. We choose the right fix based on your layout, soil conditions, slope, and how the property is being used. The goal is not a quick patch. We want a drainage system that works with your landscape and protects your investment.

Good drainage is also important when planning larger outdoor projects. If you are adding a patio, retaining wall, concrete feature, or a full backyard renovation, drainage should be handled the right way from the start. Our team brings hands-on experience in landscaping and hardscaping installations, so we understand how drainage connects to the long-term performance of the entire space.

We work with homeowners across the Twin Cities who want practical solutions and clean workmanship. Whether you have pooling water near the house, a muddy side yard, or runoff that is damaging your lawn, we can assess the problem and recommend a clear next step. If you want to protect your yard, foundation, and outdoor living areas with a drainage plan that is built to last, contact Lake County Outdoors to schedule a consultation.
