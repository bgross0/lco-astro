At Lake County Outdoors, property maintenance means keeping your site safe, clean, and looking sharp in every season. For many of our clients across the Minneapolis metro, that starts with winter. We put a strong focus on commercial snow removal and ice management because dependable winter service protects your customers, employees, tenants, and daily operations.

We actively service commercial properties this winter with seasonal maintenance agreements built around the needs of each site. That can include snow plowing, lot clearing, sidewalk snow removal, ice monitoring, and deicing support for safer walkways, drive lanes, and parking areas. We know Minnesota winters can change fast, so our goal is simple: keep your property accessible and reduce weather-related hazards before they become bigger problems.

Our team brings 10+ years of combined hands-on experience in outdoor property care. We work with commercial property managers who need reliable snow and ice service, and we also help homeowners who want consistent upkeep and a polished outdoor space. If you manage an office, retail site, multifamily property, or other commercial location, we can tailor a winter property maintenance plan to your layout, traffic flow, and risk areas.

Outside of snow season, property maintenance can also include general landscape upkeep that supports curb appeal and long-term property value. As a full-service landscaping company, we understand how all parts of an exterior space work together. Our broader experience includes landscaping, hardscaping, tree work, and site improvements, which helps us spot issues early and maintain your property with a practical, experienced eye.

When we take on a property, we focus on consistency, communication, and quality work. We want you to feel confident that your grounds are being maintained by a crew that shows up prepared and understands the expectations of professional site care. Whether you need winter snow and ice management for a commercial property or dependable year-round outdoor maintenance, we’re ready to help.

If you are looking for property maintenance in Minneapolis or the Twin Cities metro, contact Lake County Outdoors to discuss your site, your priorities, and the right service plan for this season.
