At Lake County Outdoors, we build fences that do more than mark a property line. A well-built fence adds privacy, improves security, keeps kids and pets safer, and gives your yard a clean, finished look. We work with homeowners across the Minneapolis metro who want fencing that fits the style of the home and holds up through Minnesota weather.

We install fencing for many goals, including backyard privacy, pool areas, pet containment, decorative borders, and added security. Whether you want a simple layout or a fence that ties into a larger outdoor living project, we help you choose an option that makes sense for your property, budget, and long-term use. Because we also handle landscaping, hardscaping, tree work, and full outdoor transformations, we can make sure your fence works with the rest of your yard instead of looking like an afterthought.

Our fencing service starts with a site review and planning. We look at the slope of the yard, access points, existing landscaping, drainage concerns, and how the fence should function day to day. From there, we map out the layout, gate placement, and overall design. We focus on practical details that matter, like clean lines, strong post placement, and spacing that supports both appearance and durability.

We can help with common fencing styles used for residential properties, including privacy fencing, decorative fencing, and boundary fencing. Homeowners often choose fencing to create a quieter backyard feel, improve curb appeal, and add value to the property. For larger projects, fencing can also be part of a complete dream backyard build with patios, retaining features, or other custom outdoor improvements.

Our team brings 10+ years of combined hands-on experience in outdoor projects, and we take pride in doing solid work that lasts. We are a full-service landscaping company, so we understand how fencing fits into the bigger picture of a yard’s layout, function, and appearance. That means better coordination, fewer surprises, and a finished result that feels intentional.

If you are planning a new fence in Minneapolis or the Twin Cities metro, we are ready to help. Contact Lake County Outdoors to talk through your project and get a plan built around your property.
