When a tree becomes unsafe, diseased, storm-damaged, or simply in the way of your plans, we handle the removal safely and efficiently. At Lake County Outdoors, we provide professional tree removal for homeowners across the Minneapolis metro, with a practical, hands-on approach built on 10+ years of combined experience in outdoor projects. Our goal is simple: protect your property, remove the hazard, and leave the area clean and ready for what comes next.

We remove trees for many common reasons, including dead or dying trees, heavy lean, storm damage, cracked trunks, root problems, overgrown canopies, and trees growing too close to homes, garages, driveways, fences, or utility areas. We also help when you need space cleared for landscaping upgrades, patio construction, boulder walls, concrete work, or a full backyard redesign.

Our tree removal service starts with a close look at the tree, its size, condition, and location. We assess nearby structures, overhead obstacles, access points, and the safest removal method for the site. From there, we create a clear plan to take the tree down in controlled sections when needed. This helps reduce risk to your home, yard, and surrounding landscape.

Depending on the project, our work may include section-by-section dismantling, limb removal, trunk cutting, debris hauling, and site cleanup. We focus on safe work practices, efficient equipment use, and careful handling of the surrounding property. If nearby landscaping, hardscaping, or fencing needs to be protected during the job, we plan for that as part of the process.

Because we are a full-service outdoor company, we can also help after the tree is gone. Many customers ask us to regrade the area, improve the yard layout, install new landscape features, or prepare the space for a dream backyard project. That means you do not have to juggle multiple contractors to move from removal to improvement.

If you have a tree that looks risky, damaged, or overdue for removal, now is the time to address it before it causes bigger problems. Contact Lake County Outdoors to discuss your tree removal project and get a clear plan for safe, quick, and reliable service.
