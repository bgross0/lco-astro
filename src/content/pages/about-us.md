At Lake County Outdoors, we help property owners in the Minneapolis metro get more from their outdoor spaces and winter operations. We are a full-service landscaping company with a strong focus on **commercial snow removal and ice management**, along with custom hardscaping, tree work, and large outdoor improvement projects. Our goal is simple: do dependable work, communicate clearly, and make your property safer, cleaner, and easier to manage in every season.

We bring more than 10 years of combined hands-on experience to each job. Our team works with residential homeowners who want a standout backyard and with commercial property managers who need reliable winter service. While we build high-end patios, boulder walls, concrete features, and complete outdoor spaces, we are especially focused on helping commercial properties stay open and safe through Minnesota winters.

### Built for Minnesota Properties

Living and working in the Twin Cities means we know what local properties deal with. Heavy snow, ice buildup, freeze-thaw cycles, drainage issues, and wear on hard surfaces all affect how outdoor spaces need to be built and maintained. We plan our work around real conditions here, whether we are installing a dream backyard or managing snow and ice for a commercial site.

For winter clients, that means practical service, fast response, and seasonal planning that fits the needs of the property. We typically work through **seasonal snow maintenance agreements** with terms based on the client and site. This approach helps commercial property managers stay ready all winter with a clear plan for snow plowing, ice control, and site access.

### Our Approach

We believe good service starts with listening. Every property is different, and every customer has different priorities. Some need a custom patio and retaining wall that transforms the yard. Others need a snow contractor who shows up consistently and helps reduce winter headaches. We take the time to understand the site, the scope, and the end goal so we can recommend the right solution.

We also believe in doing the work the right way. That means using the right equipment, planning for site conditions, and keeping communication straightforward from the first conversation through completion. Whether the project is a boulder wall, concrete installation, tree trimming, or ongoing snow and ice service, we focus on work that holds up and service you can count on.

### More Than Landscaping

Even though we are known for creating beautiful outdoor spaces, winter service is a major part of what we do. Commercial snow removal is about more than plowing a lot. It is about keeping entrances accessible, reducing slip hazards, supporting tenant and customer safety, and helping businesses operate with less disruption. That is why we put a strong emphasis on snow and winter services for commercial properties across the Minneapolis metro.
